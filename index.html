Meet Marci Metzger

Marci Metzger is a highly accomplished real estate broker and the owner of The Ridge Realty Group, bringing over 30 years of industry expertise to the Southern Nevada market. Since beginning her career in 1995, she has established herself as a top-tier professional, specializing in residential and commercial sales, land development, and luxury relocations within Pahrump, Mountain Falls, Las Vegas, and Henderson. Her reputation is built on high-stakes negotiation skills and a deep-rooted commitment to her community, consistently ranking her among the top residential agents in the region.
